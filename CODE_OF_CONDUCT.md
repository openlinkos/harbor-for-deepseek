# Code of Conduct

Harbor is a small community project. People who participate in issues, pull requests, and discussions are expected to:

- Be respectful. Disagree about the product, not about the person.
- Keep API keys, passphrases, and private conversations out of public posts.
- Avoid harassment, slurs, and sexual attention that the other person did not ask for. This applies to everyone, including people who are new and people who maintain the repo.
- Follow the branding rules in the README. Do not present Harbor as an official product.

Maintainers may edit or hide comments, and may block further participation, when a post breaks these rules. Report a conduct problem the same way as a security problem: the private security contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Include the public link and what you would like to happen. Do not include secrets.
